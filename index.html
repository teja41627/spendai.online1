Security, Architecture & Privacy FAQ

Where is my spending data saved?

Every single rupee input and transaction you record is saved directly inside your own web browser's storage (localStorage). It never travels to any server, third-party trackers, or databases.

Is SpendAI truly free?

Yes, 100% free with absolutely no hidden tiers. Since the application runs completely client-side in your browser, we do not require costly database servers. This enables us to make SpendAI totally free forever.

How does the smart category suggestion work?

Our instant keyword engine intercepts keys inside the description prompt. Entering words like "Swiggy", "Zomato", or "restaurant" immediately matches "Food & Dining", while terms like "recharge", "Jio", or "Airtel" flags "Mobile Recharge", saving you administrative steps.
