About SpendAI

SpendAI is a free, local-first, privacy-focused financial suite designed to give you complete visibility over where your money goes. Built directly inside your web browser, SpendAI requires no bank logins, no phone validations, and zero subscriptions.

Start Budgeting Free Contact Our Support

Our Vision

We envision a web where users do not have to exchange their private bank transaction statements, logins, or identities just to build a simple monthly budget. Financial tools should serve the citizen, not compile data points for advertising companies. SpendAI aims to prove that premium-tier financial planning software can run completely client-side in the browser.

Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize high-fidelity financial budgeting and expense optimization for the modern Indian earner. By supporting domestic frameworks (such as INR tracking, local UPI categorizations, and subscription leakage auditing), we help users cancel unnecessary recurring debts, compound index fund savings, and establish secure financial foundations.

Four Pillars of SpendAI

Engineered client-side with privacy-preserving technologies.

Local-First Sandbox

No cloud syncing, no databases, and no login required. Your data remains strictly inside your browser sandbox.

Heuristic Categorization

An instant client-side keyword parser that recognizes brands like Swiggy, Uber, Zomato, and Jio to auto-tag categories.

50/30/20 Budget Rule

Dynamic sliders to allocate monthly salaries across Needs, Wants, and Savings, tracking limits in real-time.

Subscription Auditor

A tracker that flags recurring leaks (broadband plans, unutilized streaming platforms) to save you money annually.
